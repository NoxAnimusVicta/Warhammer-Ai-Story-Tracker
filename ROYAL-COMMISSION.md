# Royal technical commission — House Orsival

**Accepted and authorised: 11/10/0068 AC43, evening, exchanges 540–543.** This is a technical commission with direct reporting access to Margrave Odrienne Orcemont, not the standing Royal Advisor appointment and not command of Veyrasse’s armed forces.

## Remit and authority

Galahad will direct development in military engineering and practical applications of the expedition’s findings. The Margrave grants access to relevant military works, assigned specialists and a Chancery liaison within the commissioned undertaking. Marshal Calvren Vaucerin and Lieutenant-General Cevrel Darscelet support the programme and recommend him for future advisory office.

The sovereign will review the **Royal Advisor** appointment after a first rifle trial shipment has been produced and demonstrated under controlled conditions. Winning a war or capturing a position is not a prerequisite. Galahad proposed personally taking a trained, discreet unit through battlefield use; any later field deployment and operational authority require military command’s agreement. The Cressault armistice still applies. Secrecy is an objective, not a guarantee.

Intended for ordinary infantry, with a full-power long .30-calibre cartridge, ten-round box magazine, wooden furniture, perforated shroud and folding bipod. There is no tested performance, issued shipment or production already underway. The separate contemplated .50/.60 personal derivative remains undecided and unbuilt.
- **Airborne Carrier Command Vessel:** preliminary airborne and seagoing aircraft/troop carrier concept with rotating propulsion. A substantial research and industrial programme is required; no working vessel, approved construction budget or operational capability exists. Development may proceed alongside the rifle subject to a defined resource case.

The original drawings remain Galahad’s. Production rights require an explicit agreement. The portfolio was shown privately to the Lieutenant-General, Marshal and Margrave, not deposited among the general expedition papers being transcribed. The earlier monumental civil designs remain unbuilt and separately unfunded.
