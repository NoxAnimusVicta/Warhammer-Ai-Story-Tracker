## Controlling rule: research carries forward

New projects inherit all relevant established discoveries, tested principles, materials processes, components, tools and manufacturing capability. Estimate effort against the remaining unfamiliar work. Never require Galahad to unlock the same technology again for each different product, mounting or application.

His exceptional cognition, engineering specialisation and ability to connect disciplines should produce pronounced cumulative progress. Understanding one system can accelerate several others. Shared foundations do not automatically solve unrelated requirements, and an inference remains distinct from a tested result. Do not invent new mysterious barriers merely to delay a breakthrough.

## Track achievements separately

| Stage | Evidence of achievement |
|---|---|
| Operate | Reliably use the existing device. |
| Understand | Develop and test an explanation of its important functions and interactions. |
| Maintain and adapt | Diagnose faults, perform demonstrated repairs, or integrate existing components into a different application. |
| Reproduce | Build a working equivalent without consuming irreplaceable captured components. |
| Industrialise | Trained people other than Galahad can repeatedly manufacture and maintain it at established quality and cost. |

These are descriptive milestones, not a rigid sequence or universal technology level. Record both theoretical understanding and production capability: understanding a captured component is valuable without implying its independent manufacture. A successful hybrid using captured parts is a real achievement, with that dependency retained.

## Assess difficulty by the actual gaps

For each project record: unfamiliar theory; missing observations or experimental evidence; required materials; required fabrication and measurement capability; and any psychic dependency. Include the available specimen, access, prior related research, personnel and resources when estimating time.

Galahad can greatly accelerate analysis, experiment design and tool development. Intelligence does not grant observations he has never obtained. An intact object can reveal operation without uniquely revealing its manufacturing history. Appropriate experimentation or alternative materials can resolve that gap; it is not an automatic permanent prohibition.

A personal psychic-assisted prototype may precede an independently functioning version. Record the exact demonstrated dependency and who can operate it. Potential psychic engineering is not an automatically mastered technique, nor does a personal success mean ordinary soldiers can use an equivalent device unaided.

## Hunter plasma technology — adopted scope and conditional estimates

Hunters possess shoulder-mounted plasma casters alongside other plasma weapons. Predator provides the visual and functional inspiration; these remain the campaign's original regional xenos using technology adapted to the 40K setting, not an import of every Predator-franchise technical claim.

The original platform of the isolated emitter examined at Drossane remains unresolved: rifle, shoulder caster or shared weapon family are possible. Preserve the original encounter. Galahad did not acquire a complete functioning plasma weapon, its power supply or a manufacturing archive there. His emitter study and subsequent research are genuine foundations, not complete plasma manufacture.

The following are campaign planning bands for present Galahad, in local elapsed time, assuming unrestricted study of a functioning example with its necessary supply, controlled tests and appropriate research support. They are not canon specifications, completed work or automatic countdowns.

| Research target | Conditional planning band |
|---|---|
| Establish operation and identify major functional systems | Hours to a few days |
| Substantial tested understanding of the complete weapon | Approximately 1–4 weeks |
| Useful maintenance or adaptation retaining advanced Hunter components | Several weeks to a few months, depending on the change |
| Independently manufactured equivalent | Months to years; 6–24 months is an illustrative band only where resources and missing industrial processes can be developed |
| Hunter-level compactness, reliability and repeatable manufacture | Assess separately from demonstrated prototype and supply-chain progress |

Do not depict years of empty incomprehension as the default. A working example should produce substantial early insight. Conversely, the table does not award unavailable industrial facilities or compel success by a deadline.

### Rifle to shoulder caster

Relevant plasma principles, materials, power systems, thermal management, controls, diagnostics, tools and quality methods carry over. When the designs are encountered, establish whether they share actual components, only underlying principles, or both, and preserve that decision consistently.

The additional work concerns the particular mounting, movement, targeting interface and integration with the wearer, plus any genuinely changed size or power requirements. A first derivative may plausibly take weeks to months after independent rifle manufacture is achieved. Sophisticated independent tracking or unfamiliar sensors can require separate research. This is additional work, not another full plasma-development period.

Materials and power discoveries may also advance civilian machinery, transport and the carrier programme where relevant. Record the transferred contribution without declaring those entire projects solved.

## Lore distinctions informing the framework

There is no canonical universal invention timer established by the sources below. These campaign bands express Galahad's agreed capabilities and circumstances, not a setting-wide research tree.

- **Szeras:** GW credits him with realising biotransference using knowledge supplied by the C'tan, while describing his continuing pursuit of the deepest secrets of life. Implementing that process is distinct from understanding every aspect of life or reversing its losses. Do not summarise this as simply forgetting his own machine. [Biotransference](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/10vZnrg0/what-is-biotransference/); [continuing research](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/k3n4KfMr/psychic-awakening-for-every-end-a-beginning/).
- **Necron inference:** the ability to defeat or fragment an entity does not logically establish the ability to reverse biotransference or restore what was lost. Even intact memory does not make every individual an expert in every field. This is reasoning about the comparison, not a newly sourced claim about the Silent King's exact memories.
- **Iron Hands:** the Keys of Hel concern forbidden technologies including cybernetic resurrection, recovered and suppressed before later use. This does not establish identical mechanisms or outcomes to Necron biotransference, or independent invention of the whole science by ordinary Iron Fathers. *The Horus Heresy Book Two: Massacre*, printed p. 91; John French's *Riven* and *The Keys of Hel*. [Black Library anthology listing](https://www.blacklibrary.com/all-products/hh-shattered-legions-ebook.html). The discussion used the published source passage and book listings, not a full reread of those stories.
- **Cawl, Bile and STCs:** exceptional innovation is possible, but success does not establish a short development time. *Genefather* explicitly involves seeking help to unlock the pylon network. An STC is not a supernatural requirement for every invention; missing knowledge, processes, resources and institutional constraints are separate obstacles. [Genefather](https://www.blacklibrary.com/warhammer-40000/novels/ebook-genefather-eng-2023.html); [GW's scientific-minds discussion](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/ANRfbUr5/genefather-fabius-bile-is-back-to-test-his-wits-against-the-biggest-brains-in-the-galaxy/); [Mechanicus overview](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/HUNPAc0m/starting-an-adeptus-mechanicus-army-in-warhammer-40000-everything-you-need-to-know-from-painting-to-lore/).
