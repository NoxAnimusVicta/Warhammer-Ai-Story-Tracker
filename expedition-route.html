How to use each stay

Fourteen destinations receive six full days and eight receive five. A six-day visit allows one day for introductions and access, three for parallel research, one for follow-up and one for consolidation. Five-day visits combine the last two stages. Arrival and travel days are additional, not counted as full research days. Five scholars work in parallel; Galahad keeps separate appointment blocks for private meetings and engineering interests. These stays support reconnaissance and selected relationships, not exhaustive study of every city.

Departure arrangements

  • Funding secured: 10,000 lorrats from the Chancery plus 4,500 from the Collegium. The operating fund holds 13,990 after 510 paid, including 2,444 contingency. The Collegium separately continues Galahad’s 60/month and the five scholars’ 35/month each: 2,820 annually. Total cash provision 17,320. Loan instruments and home administrative support are additional institutional resources. Itemised budget and calculations.
  • Season planned: departure is in northern autumn. The southern crossing on days 114–138 falls in southern summer. Northern calls during days 227–269 fall in the late-spring/summer service window; the Iskel–Norrak passage on days 313–336 falls inside its days 295–350 summer/early-autumn window, including room for the ten-day margin. Ordinary winter ice closure has been planned around.
  • Timetables and bookings: agents’ connections and carrier arrangements cover the allocated windows, based on 480km/day effective rail/sea progress and 200km/day road progress with rest and transfers. First tickets are paid; onward ticketing is institutionally guaranteed and reconfirmed locally, not all prepaid. Paid lodging and appropriate road hire are arranged throughout.
  • Deadline: return target day 355; hard deadline 365 days from departure. The 21 preparation days do not consume the travel allowance. Ten reserve days remain protected.
  • Transport: six travellers, instruments and freight; Galahad’s berth, access and road vehicle account for his current size and growth to 3 m. Ordinary scholars retain human rest schedules.
  • Permissions: ordinary entry, transit, declared instruments and routine academic visits are arranged for all 22 principal stops and intermediate jurisdictions. Guarded military sites, private arcane collections and restricted exports need individual consent; they are optional research objectives, not unresolved departure conditions.
  • Payments and support: the bursar maintains payment orders, correspondent agents, local conversion and replenishment. No reliance on foreign gifts, universal acceptance of lorrats or Galahad’s personal estate funds. Savel receives duplicate reports; Tessier maintains travel papers and correspondence.
  • Scope:22 capitals/principal seats, including Ossendrienne as a seat of successor territories. This does not promise every city, divided authority or outer-island administration. Shipping calls are not automatically research stays.

Protecting the return

Keep the ten-day reserve uncommitted. Review actual progress at Tervessac and Orsevigne before the next ocean stage. At Grevallier, the agent reconfirms the arranged homeward passage and current ice notice. If a new disruption threatens the deadline, shorten remaining research stays in time and bring any material route decision to Galahad. Routine seasonal closure is already accounted for; no automatic flight is assumed.
