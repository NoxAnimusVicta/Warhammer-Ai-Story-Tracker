# Galahad — controlling physiology reference

Revision 46 · Accepted endurance and comparative-capability clarification. This is narrator continuity for this campaign, not a universal canonical specification for Custodes or Primarchs. It supersedes older conflicting sleep, stamina and overall combat assumptions, including revision43 and the preparation scene's 3–4-hour sleep statement. Historical dialogue remains unchanged.

## Sleep and wakefulness

Approximately **seven hours of restorative sleep per seven local days** maintains optimal condition. One weekly block or roughly one hour daily are both suitable. Missing a day or a week is not a fatigue trigger. These agreed sleep provisions apply to ongoing narration now; do not defer them automatically until his third birthday.

Galahad can remain awake for months while performing vastly above human standards. After approximately **six to twelve local months without sleep**, mild deprivation can begin gradually. This is neither a mandatory symptom at six months nor a collapse, psychosis or incapacitation deadline at twelve. He can continue beyond it, with impairment judged against his own exceptional baseline. Do not quietly restore daily Astartes sleep requirements or a fourteen-day wakefulness ceiling through partial-neural-rest terminology.

Sustained psychic self-restoration could potentially permit indefinite wakefulness. This is an accepted developmental possibility, not an already demonstrated technique or automatic passive effect. Capacity to develop an application is distinct from having mastered it.

## Stamina, injury and psychic capacity

Endurance and resilience substantially exceed ordinary Astartes and Primaris baselines. Weeks, and potentially months, of sustained campaigning and repeated fighting with little degradation are appropriate to his established potential. Continuous maximum-output combat is more demanding, but that distinction must not become a pretext for routine Marine-scale exhaustion. An endurance estimate does not eliminate supplies, ammunition or actual damage.

Sleep deprivation, muscular expenditure, injury and psychic exertion are related burdens, not interchangeable timers. Apply Galahad's exceptional reserves to all of them. Fatigue requires a concrete burden proportionate to his capabilities; unfamiliarity can affect finesse without implying depleted reserves.

Assess wounds by the damage actually sustained and its functional consequences. Reinforcement, physiological redundancy, rapid sealing and pain tolerance mean a wound that seriously impairs a Marine may barely impede him. Visible injury or pain alone is not evidence of lost fighting effectiveness. Major structural damage and sufficiently powerful attacks remain consequential; durability is not absolute invulnerability.

His stable body–soul integration and exceptional psychic potential provide substantially greater power capacity and endurance than an average psyker or ordinary Space Marine Librarian. Routine use of familiar telekinesis, calming influence or other established abilities must not repeatedly exhaust him. Capacity, precision, range and learned applications remain distinct. No new mastered biomancy, universal telepathy or unrelated power is awarded by this clarification.

## Physical comparison and maturity

Unaided physical performance exceeds ordinary Astartes; being below a typical Custodian in overall unaided physical performance does not impose an Astartes ceiling on sleep, durability, stamina or psychic reserves. Evaluate each established faculty rather than deriving every attribute from a single ranking.

At **full maturity**, one typical Custodian is insufficient to reliably defeat Galahad under comparable conditions, considering his combined physical and psychic capabilities. More than one would ordinarily be needed to put him down. Experience, developed powers and equipment can increase that advantage; no fixed opponent-count formula overrides tactics, conditions or exceptional individuals. Do not turn his current lack of experience into permanently reduced potential, or award future experience immediately.

He remains a perfected proto-Primarch, modestly above the adopted Alpha Primus overall benchmark and below a true Primarch overall. Full bodily maturity occurs around three local years at three metres. Current approximate age remains two years and three months; the last actual measurement remains 2.59 metres. This clarification advances no time, combat, training, transactions or demonstrated powers.

## Narration and maintenance rule

Read this reference before resolving prolonged activity, fatigue, injury or comparative combat. It takes precedence over older physiology estimates and historical scene wording. Maintain the distinction between established performance, agreed mature potential and demonstrated techniques. Do not reopen accepted campaign specifications merely because canon lacks an exact numerical counterpart. Published lore claims require their own evidence; these agreed figures stand independently as campaign continuity.

Food and water estimates are unchanged by this sleep clarification. Historical records of choosing to sleep remain valid voluntary behaviour, not proof of the superseded biological requirement. New contrary limits require discussion with the player rather than an unnoticed regression to ordinary human or Astartes assumptions.
