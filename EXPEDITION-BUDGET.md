## Accounting and assumptions

- Chancery operating contribution: 10,000. Collegium operating contribution: 4,500. Collegium ordinary payroll commitment: 2,820. Total Collegium cash provision: 7,320. This supersedes both the original 6,000 grant and the later 6,000/8,500 discussion proposal.
- Operating payments already made: 510. Operating balance before departure: **13,990**. Of that, 11,546 covers forecast remaining costs and 2,444 is contingency. The earlier 2,100 salary earmark is removed; it is not a second receipt or extra cash. Personal funds and estate accounts remain unchanged.
- The annual model allows 73 shipboard days/nights and 292 days/nights ashore: 365 in all, including the ten contingency days. Sea fares include board and lodging, so those services are not charged again ashore. This is an aggregate budgeting split based on roughly 35,015 sea km at the itinerary’s 480 km/day underway assumption; final timetables can redistribute nights.
- The existing rail tariff is 0.008–0.018 L/passenger-km; 0.014 is a respectable working level within that band. Extra space is budgeted as seven fare-equivalents for six actual passengers; it does not mean a seventh traveller or a guaranteed seventh seat satisfying every vehicle layout.
- Sea fares, hotel room prices and long-distance vehicle packages were not previously established tariffs. The explicit rates above are newly calibrated campaign budgeting assumptions. They are not discovered historical quotes or universal foreign prices. The road package extends the existing 3–6 L/half-day local light-truck band with a separate long-distance supplement.
- Accommodation is paid, clean professional lodging with secure instrument storage, rather than royal suites. Host-provided rooms or facilities are welcome savings; none are needed to make the budget balance. Galahad’s knowledge, patronage or future earnings are not monetised to fund the plan.
- Borrowed Collegium instruments are existing institutional assets, not newly purchased expenses. Galahad’s established regular salary is 60 per pay month, evidenced by the earlier six-month stipend of 360 and a subsequent monthly payment of 60. It continues on ordinary payroll; no new leadership premium or future personal cash receipt is invented. Institutional administration, correspondence, introductions and loan equipment are supplied in kind from existing resources, without an invented additional cash valuation.
- At 0.018 rather than 0.014 for every remaining railway place, rail costs rise by about 1,250 L. The reserve covers that variation with about 1,194 left. It is not a guarantee against every simultaneous worst case; more severe disruptions require ordinary institutional support or itinerary adjustment.
- Reserve funds are accessible through the expedition’s institutional travel arrangements and held apart from discretionary expenditure. Do not narrate the party as dependent on foreign benefactors for passage home. Actual bills reduce cash when paid; unspent reserve is returned/accounted for.

The correction establishes competent joint sponsorship during the already completed preparation interval. It adds no elapsed story time and does not change the route, permissions already granted, Galahad’s authority, or the settled 510 of purchases.
