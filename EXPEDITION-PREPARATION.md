## Money access, communications and return decisions

The bursar has arranged letters of credit/payment orders through the expedition's commercial and academic correspondents, with local cash conversion and scheduled replenishment at principal stops. The lorrat is an accounting equivalent, not legal tender everywhere. Actual conversions and fees are entered when paid. The party does not carry the entire annual budget in coin or need foreign donations to reach its next port.

The reserve remains available through those arrangements, with duplicate authorisations retained at home. Ordinary communications use post and available telegraph/telephone links; there is no assumption of instant contact on every sea leg. Tessier maintains the correspondence and papers; Galahad retains authority over material changes. The Collegium handles routine booking and payment issues within the approved budget. The medical kit, treatment allowance and contact with local practitioners cover ordinary needs without making Galahad responsible for every human illness.

At Tervessac and Orsevigne, the party compares actual progress with the route before committing to the next ocean stage. At Grevallier, the agent reconfirms the arranged homeward sailing and latest ice notice before discretionary work. Ordinary delays use the ten-day margin. If new events threaten the deadline, shorten remaining research stays before consuming the whole margin; all 22 stays have at least five full days and can release time if required. A genuinely major disruption triggers an explicit new route decision with current costs. No automatic flight or extra funding is assumed necessary for the ordinary plan.

The visit list covers 22 capitals or principal seats across the three inhabited continents. It does not promise every major city, every divided authority or every island administration. Intermediate supply calls are not automatically research stays. This is the adopted scope of this year's journey.
