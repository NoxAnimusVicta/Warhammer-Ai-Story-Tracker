# Underground complex — construction programme

Current return: **25/02/0069 AC43**. The earlier estimates below are retained as the planning basis; the approved expanded programme now controls.

The revised concept is an isolated, concealed underground installation, rather than beneath the Orsival estate. Indicative scope: 20,000–30,000 square metres over four to six levels, 150–250 permanent occupants and 100–150 additional temporary occupants. The earlier 3–8 million-lorrat, two-to-four-year estimate is a preliminary conventional schedule, not a quote for the accelerated programme.

## Approved expanded programme

On **24/11/0068**, the Crown authorised **14 million lorrats**: the prior 11-million illustrative scope plus 2.2 million for remote rail yards/underground access and 0.8 million for additional protection/airstrip allowances. These remain planning allowances within a ceiling, not fixed tender prices. Work began **27/11/0068**; completion deadline **19/11/0069 AC43**, one year from the instruction.

The site is an unnamed isolated Crown tract in inland uplands near Auvrienne, not beneath the estate. Several small rail yards kilometres away will feed underground supply lines; the airstrip has restricted nocturnal use planned. Deep accommodation is intended to withstand sustained heavy bombardment. No depth measurement, completed protective performance or operational transport connection is yet certified.
