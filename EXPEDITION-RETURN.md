# Completed expedition — return and research record

**Returned 11/10/0068 AC43, day 355.** Departure was 22/10/0067; the day-365 deadline remains 21/10/0068. The party completed the established circuit of 22 capitals and principal seats across three continents: 231 travel days and 124 full visiting days. Ten reserve days were unused. Surface travel, seasonal passages and permissions were honoured; no flight or itinerary drift was enacted. All five scholars returned uninjured.

Galahad’s comparative study encompasses naval architecture, shipyards, aircraft, arms, vehicles, materials, infrastructure, cultivation, history, religion, culture, strategy, diplomacy, commerce and the older arts. He has absorbed and cross-connected the scholars’ work with his own observations; these are substantial learned advances, not unread notes. Extraordinary synthesis is not omniscience or automatic mastery of every secret process.

Official reports were inventoried at the Collegium and transcription for the Chancery began on return. The Headmaster, faculty and Chancery received civil-engineering briefings covering architectural improvements, shipyards, airports, aqueducts, desalination and sewage recycling. No national installation or realised revenue follows merely from presenting a design. Copying and faculty review are delegated; Savel will consolidate substantive questions in a letter. Galahad is not required to remain for another meeting tomorrow. The Headmaster remains unnamed.

The five scholars increasingly regard him as a mentor. Academic competition and his approval motivate them without eliminating their judgement: Osselin Varcet (mechanics), Iselle Rouvant (chemistry/materials), Elvire Sarnot (survey/waterworks), Rovain Tessier (languages/history/administration), Darsenne Aubrel (natural history).

## Private research and possessions

Thousands of private pages record engineering and psychic techniques, observations, focusing methods and synthesis. The developed cipher uses three reversible transformations: a first invented language expressing conceptual relationships, a culinary-recipe intermediate, and a second invented language as the final layer. Older sensitive notes were recast. Galahad writes it fluently; no adversarial cryptanalysis has established that it is unbreakable. Decisive private work remains separate from the useful official findings supplied to the institutions.

Hunter studies added supervised fragments and records to the Drossane emitter hypothesis, narrowing likely functions and exploring missing power/control problems. There is no acquired Hunter component, functional weapon, proven alien material recipe or plasma prototype. The Drossane assembly remains Lucien Sarvelle’s office property; the separate Miravelle lead has no documented acquisition.

Two original collections were recovered and retained: wrapped practical psychic observations from a ruined teaching house near Ossendrienne, and engraved plates with commentary from a disused mountain repository near Grevallier. Telekinesis enabled access through unstable masonry. Neither collection has proven Ambros authorship. Visits and comparisons in Veldrassen and Ostrevain expanded knowledge of his legacy without resolving the circumstances of his sword killing or recovering his sword.

Private manuscripts and both recovered collections are separated in new ground-floor Collegium quarters overlooking a high-walled court, with a long table and custom bed. Galahad can stand inside but must duck at the doorway. Earlier stored belongings and the wrapped Vouressan-bone greatsword were moved there. He is unarmed at the palace and carries the original rifle/carrier presentation portfolio. Loan instruments remain Collegium property. Station papers and the old incomplete firearm prototype remain in Corva’s custody; no transfer is implied.
