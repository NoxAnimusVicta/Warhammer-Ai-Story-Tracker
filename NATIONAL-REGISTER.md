# Malaspina — national staff returns

Return: Ten months and eleven days after Collegium enrolment — evening

Rounded estimates. The numerical source is national-register.json.

## Valuation

Output is annual domestic value added, expressed at a common local purchasing-power valuation. It is neither treasury cash nor foreign currency exchange. Local currencies remain distinct.

## Uncertainty

Rounded staff estimates: national output and personnel commonly vary by about 15 percent; equipment and fragmented jurisdictions by 25 percent or more. No estimate guarantees an outcome.

## Forces

Standing personnel include active services, permanent garrisons, cadres and obligated household troops once. Additional trained reserves exclude serving personnel and untrained population. Sustainable field strength is the standing-force portion supportable away from fixed defences over roughly ninety days on established supply routes; it includes support staff and is not all riflemen.

## Equipment

Armour means serviceable tanks and armoured cars, not all military vehicles. Aircraft are combat, reconnaissance and maritime types, excluding trainers. Artillery excludes infantry mortars. Fleet displacement totals commissioned combat vessels including coastal and river craft, excluding merchant ships; tonnage alone does not establish fighting quality.

## Technology

1: basic local repair; 2: established older production; 3: mature current manufacture; 4: advanced specialist production; 5: leading reproducible practice. Ratings measure industrial depth, not access to every invention. Readiness, logistics and cohesion use 1 (weak) to 5 (exceptional).

## Coverage

Food and fuel coverage compare normal domestic annual supply to normal domestic consumption; above 100 percent permits exports. These are not stored months of supply, and import partners or transport may fail. National food and fuel returns reconcile against planetary production. Fuel is coal-equivalent energy and food is grain-equivalent nourishment; these aggregates do not make fuel grades or foods interchangeable. Distribution losses and reserve replenishment absorb the modest global surplus.

## Scope

Ossavren and smaller-jurisdiction entries are geographic sums across separate authorities, not united states. Cressault is already within Veyrasse population and resources; the disputed corridor has no additional national army or treasury. Serevask counts loyal districts only.

## Updates

Return epoch matches the current census. Annual output trend describes ordinary net capacity change, not guaranteed growth. War, closures, purges, investment, losses and political changes require revised returns.
