Their shielding helps against comparable interference, not Galahad's overwhelming deliberate attention. The common syllabus records conditions, repeatability and mistakes. Their differing arrival dates mean different training durations, approximately two months for Mirelle and three weeks for Mathieu.

Current priority: study and discreet infiltration through useful civilian positions. Wider recruitment and more active operations await the completed headquarters. Galahad will guide them to it when ready. His declared intention to bring Veyrasse under their control is an ambition, not an enacted takeover.
