# Malaspina — campaign data-slate

A read-only companion to an ongoing Warhammer 40,000 fan campaign, designed for iPhone 14 Pro Max. The narrator maintains the character and chronicle and publishes updates at story milestones or at the player's request.

Open https://noxanimusvicta.github.io/Warhammer-Ai-Story-Tracker/ in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. Open online once to prepare the offline archive.

The app checks the publication fingerprint when opened, on returning to view, after connectivity returns and once a minute while visible. A new edition refreshes the record. Updates cannot arrive while closed or offline. A Receive latest transmission button provides a manual check. No editing, importing or exporting is required. Older device-local character saves are ignored; the published record is authoritative.

The adult portrait is a projected appearance reference, not acquired equipment. The character has taken no actions in play. The wider experimental programme is original campaign lore; details of other subjects are not included in this public record.

Publishing source: GitHub Pages, main branch, root. Changes to the separate JSON files alone do not rebuild the HTML: publish index.html, seed.json and release.json together after rebuilding. Preserve the service worker and install assets. The embedded HTML includes the full public character state, world text and research.

Verification covers JavaScript syntax, assets, source-to-embedded state, publication fingerprints, update detection, offline failure and propagation-loop protection. Physical iPhone interaction has not been tested.

Independent fan work. Warhammer 40,000 and its established characters belong to their respective rights holders. The player supplied the character premise and Malaspina's founding lore.

The agreed campaign benchmark places every proto-Primarch roughly alongside Alpha Primus, with modest variation: above a Custodian and below a true Primarch. The detailed Primarch research remains context; earlier numeric scaling is superseded. This is an original campaign rule.

The app uses the original ivory, gold and aquamarine warp-tech sigil. The original artwork is used for the Safari Home Screen icon and web manifest. The previous gold presentation variant, without surrounding mist but with the gem shine, appears in the header and personal-sigil panel. The app icon remains the untouched original. No Chaos god or established faction allegiance is implied.


The whole cohort consists of perfected proto-Primarchs: Alpha Primus-level capability with stable body/soul integration, no constant pain and no compulsory congenital malfunction. There is no fixed psychic crisis track. Fatigue and injury remain possible. Bile intends different subjects as commanders, specialist assistants or covert protectors of the New Men; their choices and fates remain open. The exact stabilisation mechanism and Pater Mutatis’s possible contribution are unresolved.


All cohort members undergo the same foundational validity trial: deployment as very young children in incubation pods to separate, carefully screened human worlds, accelerated maturation, and largely independent development. Bile assesses each subject twenty standard years after planetfall. A pass requires becoming a major planetary power through exceptional leadership, influence and capability. An Angron-like rebellion can qualify despite facing defeat; secure territory, industry and impending victory are not mandatory. Full marks mean effective planetary control, whether achieved through violence, diplomacy, subterfuge or any combination. Mere survival is insufficient. The twenty-year deadline is concealed from the subjects. Bile intends to return for assessment and retrieval at that point, with earlier collection possible for exceptional results. He expects to terminate and examine failures that offer no further value, then apply the findings to later experiments. A useful anomaly may instead be retained, repurposed or subjected to further study. Leaving a subject indefinitely is not his default plan. Arrival and enforcement remain actions within the story, subject to travel, opposition and events; no automatic death, capture or compliance is imposed. This is Bile’s original campaign protocol, not a deadline shared by the canonical Primarchs.

Bile selects worlds without known active Imperial influence or accessible Imperial institutions, teachings and usable technological legacies. He wants to observe inherited aptitudes developing under local pressures before offering his own tutelage. Screening reduces exposure; it cannot guarantee cultural isolation, genetic obedience or future loyalty. Local upbringing still shapes every subject. The worlds need not share Malaspina’s exact geography or industrial level.

Malaspina: Dark Age colony, isolated during the Age of Strife, never Imperial. Native human technology peaks at WW1–WW2 equivalents. No functioning advanced human relics, Imperial lasguns, native nuclear reactors or hidden STC industry. Rare captured hunter devices can function in human hands. Extremely rare ancient fragments are broken and unrecognisable. Psychic traditions persist without advanced mechanical components. Hunters and Bile are external exceptions; no free salvage. Active world source is malaspina-world.txt; preserve the original user attachment unchanged.


The hunters are an original regional spacefaring xenos species, inspired by the Yautja as a player-facing reference, not a canonical 40K faction or a crossover identification. Their reach encompasses Malaspina and nearby systems: small beside the Imperium, overwhelmingly vast to local human societies. Their name, detailed biology and exact weapon specifications remain unrevealed.

Rare captured hunter weapons and devices circulate as guarded trophies, military assets and objects of study. Earlier generations of human soldiers and scholars have already learned to operate some examples and partially understand a few systems. Limited repairs, adaptations and cannibalisation are possible where knowledge and parts survive; reliable manufacture of their advanced cores, power sources and specialised components is beyond the current industrial base. These are genuine functional exceptions to the native technology ceiling, not Imperial relics. No particular device is assigned to the player.

Malaspina is held in enforced technological stagnation. Human ingenuity produces advances, but recurrent hunter attacks destroy workshops, infrastructure, records and skilled communities, while recovery operations reclaim or destroy captured equipment. Concentrated alien arsenals, conspicuous use and threatening research attract particular attention. Losses repeatedly offset gains; permanent war preparations and death-world maintenance consume the resources needed for sustained development. Even without the hunters, survival would demand substantial labour. Raids are intermittent locally, but their cumulative pressure is planetary and persistent. Imperfect surveillance permits concealed caches and scattered expertise to survive. This is an established historical condition, not a guarantee that every future advance must be erased regardless of player action.
