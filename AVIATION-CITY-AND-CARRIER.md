# Aviation industrial city and Airborne Carrier Command Vessel

Status: **deferred planning reference**, accepted for preservation out of character on 29 September 2026. Campaign price reference: **12/11/0068 AC43**. No story time passes. No site, appropriation, construction order, research breakthrough or additional Crown resources are granted. The current royal audience remains unresolved as to people, premises and funds.

## Intended undertaking

Galahad envisages a new industrial city centred on aviation, capable of producing hundreds to thousands of aircraft annually and constructing several carriers simultaneously after a prototype is proven. The ambition includes on-site manufacture of aircraft, engines, specialist components and carrier structures, with utilities, transport, housing, training, maintenance and testing infrastructure. Independence from foreign production is a requirement. Domestic mines and other upstream suppliers may feed the city; this does not imply every raw resource exists within its boundary.

Several hundred aircraft per year initially and 1,000–2,000 at maturity are illustrative planning capacities within the player's broader ambition, not fixed production quotas. Aircraft type and mix remain undecided. Several concurrent carrier builds do not establish an annual carrier delivery rate.

## Lift, structure and remaining research

Under approximately Terran sea-level atmospheric conditions, ordinary helium or hydrogen supports roughly one kilogram per cubic metre after the lifting gas's own mass is deducted. The proposed gas volume offers approximately 400–750 tonnes of support before deducting structure, machinery and all carried loads. Altitude, temperature and actual local atmosphere change this figure; it is not a payload rating.

For comparison within the planning calculation, fifty aircraft averaging four tonnes already require 200 tonnes, excluding spare fuel, stores and personnel. Conventional heavy naval construction is incompatible with simply treating this shape as a Zeppelin with a carrier deck. The intended design requires advanced lightweight construction and substantial supplementary lift. Rotating propulsion units are an intended contribution, not an established solution: their power plant, fuel and machinery must enter the same mass budget. No antigravity, new material or compact advanced power system is awarded by this discussion.

Ocean landing and subsequent operation as a seagoing aircraft carrier remain part of the ambition. Buoyancy, stability, wave loads, corrosion, water landings and transition back to flight require separate validation. The pictured hull does not prove seaworthiness.

Relevant existing discoveries in materials, engines, power, controls and psychic engineering carry forward under RESEARCH-PROGRESSION.md. Galahad need not relearn established principles. His extraordinary cognition can accelerate analysis and development, while physical experiments and the missing industrial processes still require completion. The city can produce conventional aircraft while carrier research continues.

Site, materials, workforce, production mix and supplementary-lift requirements can substantially change these estimates.

Spreading the illustrative capital cost over six years gives about 36.7 million annually: 5.4% of receipts, 10.3% of defence spending or 86% of existing defence procurement. These are comparisons, not available uncommitted money or an enacted financing plan. Refresh against current national returns when revived.
