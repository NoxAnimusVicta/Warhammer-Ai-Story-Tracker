# Cohort direction and optional Malcador fragment

Revision 58 foundation, supplemented by the accepted Tyranid and family clarification · Authorial planning and spoilers, published at the player's request. This document is outside the fiction. It does not automatically confer knowledge on Galahad or other characters; Bile's explicitly established awareness of Ouroboris is distinguished below from optional future revelations. ## Accepted cohort direction

The intended surviving ensemble is Galahad and six other founders. The original number Bile seeded is unspecified; seven no longer means the entire deployment programme. Other subjects may have been retrieved, rejected or exterminated before this ensemble comes together, but their identities and outcomes have not been narrated. Do not invent a completed history merely to fill this gap.

Galahad is intended to be the last system Bile visits in the collection sequence. This explicitly supersedes the earlier collection order. The private temporal record reconciles the seven principal visits without changing Galahad's existing deployment epoch, age or established assessment interval. Additional discarded subjects' logistics are unspecified; the seven-visit circuit cannot be asserted to cover an unlimited seeded population.

The founders are complementary sovereign organisms. Galahad specialises in innovation across disciplines, especially technology, psychic abilities and their integration. Bile did not intend him to be the best pure physical killer or the highest-output psychic specialist. Expertise in invention can nevertheless produce exceptional combat applications.

At least one other founder is designed primarily for physical killing and another for maximum psychic output. Angron/Lion/Horus is the working physical template selection, with the Khan previously considered as an alternative. Magnus/Corvus Corax/Sanguinius is the provisional psychic-template recommendation, with Curze considered as a foresight alternative. The roles are accepted; the psychic donor recipe remains provisional. No new permanent names are assigned.

These mixtures are campaign design choices, not canonical gene-seed recipes. Magnus is the clearest psychic association; Corax's concealment and Curze's or Sanguinius's foresight suggest specialised traits rather than proven additive raw output. Fighting skill and psychic mastery still require development. Angron's Butcher's Nails were implants, not an inherited genetic specification. No madness, Nails, Red Thirst or replacement congenital defect is automatically installed by choosing a donor.

Bile's planned comparison includes supervised combat, controlled psychic-output measurements and assessment against each founder's intended function. Instrumentation, intervention and trauma care make recoverable injury plausible; supervision is not a guarantee he can reverse death or repair every wound. He may be ruthless, but needlessly destroying valuable specimens is not the purpose of comparative testing. A duel measures more than output, so an innovation specialist winning a fight does not itself prove greater raw psychic power.

The player intends two cohort members eventually to fall to Chaos. Preserve this as a future story direction with identities, causes and timing unresolved. It does not establish corruption now, dictate Galahad's allegiance, or predetermine every intervening encounter. The seven-survivor collection direction does not promise permanent survival thereafter.

## Tyranid timing, Bile and remaining on Malaspina

Accepted out-of-character clarification, 29 September 2026; the story remains at 06/11/0068 AC43. These are campaign provisions and future options, not new published 40K facts or knowledge automatically acquired by Galahad.

**Timing boundary:** a Tyranid invasion of the Malaspina System is optional and may occur only after Galahad first meets Bile. It must not interrupt the initial twenty-local-year trial. No arrival date, fleet strength, in-system scouting force, infestation or current invasion countdown is established. Earlier suggestions of triggering the invasion during the trial are superseded. If activated later, establish the approaching force, its circumstances and a coherent timeline; do not continually delay arrival to match Galahad's readiness.

**Bile's knowledge:** he knew of Hive Fleet Ouroboris when selecting the system and assessed that it would not interfere with his twenty-local-year experiment. Ignorance of the regional hive fleet is not the explanation for his choice. This is an accepted campaign premise, not omniscience or a guarantee of permanent safety. Bile may reveal a later threat during the assessment, potentially giving Galahad a further reason to stay. That revelation and its consequences remain optional; no conversation or decision has happened.

**Location:** use the provisional name Malaspina System. The player's turquoise-marked map places it between Dimmamar and Naogeddon, near a depicted Ouroboris incursion strand, consistent with the campaign's western Pelegeron Cluster placement in Imperium Nihilus. The schematic map does not measure separation, establish a present fleet position or supply an arrival date. Chronostrife is the Imperial dating dispute; regional temporal distortions are the relevant travel-time complication. The agreed endpoint clock ratios do not calculate an unknown interstellar journey.

**Battlefield power is not planetary defence:** Galahad's overwhelming advantage against ordinary local formations does not let him hold every front, protect every city or defend the biosphere simultaneously. Institutions, industry, armies and capable subordinates remain essential. This does not lower his established physiology or ultimate psychic potential. Malaspina currently lacks a credible independent defence against a substantial Tyranid splinter fleet without major advancement or outside assistance; the player's '9/10' description expresses severe vulnerability, not a rolled probability for every possible fleet. Personal escape and saving the population are separate challenges, neither automatically successful. Sacrificing the surface and sheltering underground is a desperate proposed remnant-survival strategy, not a proven counter or an authorised culling of the population.

No planet-scale psychic attack or safe mass-destruction technique has been learned by filing this agreement. Greater destructive potential does not automatically provide control or strategic coverage. Powerful psychic action carries contextual Warp risks, but daemon summoning is not automatic. Instruction from experienced psykers or hazardous exploration could become future learning arcs; neither a named mentor nor travelling in the Warp is a compulsory prerequisite for all development. No guaranteed mastery, corruption or encounter is assigned.

**Parents and departure:** Galahad intends to remain on Malaspina while Corva and Veskan live. He will not casually abandon them for offworld tuition when an absence could consume their remaining lives. Manipulation is a possible story mechanism only if actually developed and resolved; it is not blanket permission to force departure or kill either parent to remove the obstacle. Exact time away depends on the route, destination and temporal domains, not an automatic rule that every journey outlasts them. The narrator must preserve this motive and Galahad's agency.

**After assessment:** Bile's preferred long-term arrangement is unresolved. Travelling with him versus remaining with periodic visits has not been selected irrevocably; the player leans towards remaining. Assessment and successful participation do not mandate immediate permanent retrieval. Malaspina can remain Galahad's home, productive base and research centre. Visits by Bile or siblings, local instruction, later cohort assemblies, joint work and requests for help can develop meaningful recurring relationships. Temporary offworld travel remains possible only through an actual player decision consistent with his family commitment. Comparative tests remain intended opportunities, not completed encounters. Preserve all existing trial dates and assessment order; do not silently convert them into compulsory departure dates.

## Optional fragment: preferred form

If adopted, an incomplete remnant associated with Malcador participated in Galahad's formation during gestation. Galahad developed as one stable person; this is not a later possessing intruder, an intact resurrected Malcador, or a hidden personality destined to replace him. Corva and Veskan remain his parents and his lived identity remains his own. Memories may be genuinely absent rather than a library waiting to unlock.

The route by which a remnant survived, reached the developing organism and became integrated remains unestablished. Neither the Golden Throne nor the Great Rift is a confirmed memory-erasing or soul-delivery mechanism. The narrator must supply a coherent campaign explanation if this is ever activated, while acknowledging the addition to canon.

Do not make compatibility a competition that always selects the strongest available psyker. Circumstance, timing, integration and affinity can explain why a remnant encountered Galahad rather than the output specialist. Bile's shaping of Galahad's neural dispositions might contribute to compatibility in this hypothesis, but is not a verified soul-attraction mechanism. Transhuman biology does not by itself exclude a soul-fragment relationship; Janus is a relevant precedent. It also does not establish that Galahad is uniquely destined to receive Malcador.

The fragment may provide a distinctive resonance and unexpected capacity, without prescribing an exact power multiplier. In an activated version of this thread, the player specifically envisages Galahad eventually exceeding the purpose-built psychic specialist in **raw psychic output**, not merely winning through clever applications. Preserve that possible dramatic comparison. It need not be accessible while the hypothesised capacity is dormant, and no current power increase is enacted by filing this option.

Bile would notice a clear, repeatable deviation under comparable measurement conditions. His first conclusion should concern unexpected performance relative to his design and the cohort, not instant certainty that Malcador is present. Training, environment, integration, measurement conditions and an unknown external influence are distinct hypotheses. Recognition of an anomaly permits investigation; it is not omniscience or automatic control over Galahad.

Galahad's accepted ultimate psychic potential stands independently of this optional origin. If the thread is never used, do not reduce his potential, force an unexplained weakness or require a substitute secret entity.

## Recognition and activation

The preferred emotional encounters are with **the Lion or Roboute Guilliman**. Magnus remains another possibility but is not the mandatory first reveal. Familiar psychic resonance, manner or association may unsettle someone who knew Malcador. Neither loyalist Primarch has been established here as an infallible soul-identification device. Recognition should be uncertain and develop through circumstances rather than an automatic verdict on sight.

Guilliman might confront grief, suspicion and the practical implications of an apparent link to the old Regent. The Lion might test identity, intent and deception before accepting any resemblance. These are possible narrative approaches, not fixed dialogue, guaranteed affection, hostility, allegiance or authentication. Bile, Magnus and the loyalists may reach different interpretations of the same evidence.

The narrator has the player's permission to develop or activate this thread at a fitting future point without seeking approval for each clue. It may instead remain unused. Do not force an encounter, retroactively identify the childhood naming whisper, plant unavoidable evidence now, or declare an origin revelation solely because this note exists. If activated, record the actual evidence, who knows what, and the new status consistently across current continuity and the reader. An NPC's belief is not automatically an authorial fact. Preserve Galahad's choices and coherent identity.

## Canon basis and limits

- **Perpetuals:** regeneration and return vary; rebirth into an unrelated child with erased memories is not a general established rule. Malcador's return after Magnus killed him involved Alivia Sureka's sacrifice in *Fury of Magnus*. [Perpetual overview](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Perpetual); [Alivia Sureka](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Alivia_Sureka).
- **Emperor fragment:** *The End and the Death II* places the deliberate shedding of a soul portion before the confrontation with Horus aboard the *Vengeful Spirit*. It is not evidence that sitting on the Throne enables this. [Source-referenced account](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Fragment_of_the_Emperor%27s_Soul).
- **Malcador's final narration:** the reproduced *The End and the Death III* passage presents his ending; the parts persisting in his chosen are described as ideas. It does not confirm an escaped living fragment. Literal survival requires an additional campaign premise, and cannot be guaranteed free of tension with that ending. [Reproduced passage](https://www.reddit.com/r/40kLore/comments/1b3ucsh/the_end_and_the_death_vol_iii_the_golden_throne/).
- **Janus:** Arvida and a Magnus fragment provide an example of a soul-fragment relationship involving a transhuman and a new identity. This supports a type of possibility, not Malcador's survival or an automatic genetic matchmaking rule. [Source-referenced Arvida account](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Revuel_Arvida); [Black Library: The Last Son of Prospero](https://www.blacklibrary.com/all-products/the-last-son-of-prospero-ebook.html).
- **Donor traits:** [Corax](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Corvus_Corax), [Sanguinius](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Sanguinius), and the [official Night Lords foresight extract](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/15-01-26_thehorusheresy_black_book_extract_night_lords-2iz7zjkzzf-2yisfrpn9r.pdf) inform the discussion. None verifies these bespoke mixtures or ranks them as optimal output combinations.

Research used available excerpts, official descriptions and source-referenced secondary accounts, not a fresh complete reading of all the novels. This is a canon-inspired optional campaign addition, not a claim that published lore has revealed Malcador's return.
