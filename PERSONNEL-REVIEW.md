# Military personnel reconciliation

**Statistical return: 02/11/0068 AC43. Opening capacity return: 27/08/0067 AC43.**

Retrospective campaign estimates adopted to correct the omitted personnel review. These are rounded planning estimates tied to the existing Year 68 outcomes, not newly discovered archival musters, exact casualty lists or information automatically known to Galahad. The statistical snapshot remains 02/11; live narration has reached 05/11 and is preserved separately in the transcript.

Standing means estimated serving personnel across active services, cadres, garrisons and obligated household troops. Additional reserves exclude all serving personnel. Authorised staffing ceilings are unknown and are not inferred from the earlier estimates. Sustainable field strength includes support personnel and is a subset of standing strength supportable away from fixed defences for about ninety days. Divided returns remain separate armies; parent and colonial holdings are not double-counted.

Transfers from active service enter reserves once; reserve transfers into active service leave reserves once. Other departures include retirement, discharge, permanent incapacity, desertion and death, without pretending to know the split. Reserve eligibility losses include age, incapacity, emigration and death. Ordinary deaths are already inside demographic mortality; no additional population deduction is applied. No general mobilisation or new war is introduced. Existing personnel budgets cover these modest movements; no second cost is booked.

Each jurisdiction has an explicit net adjustment and reason. Gross turnover is a planning assumption over the full 431-day interval: normally 5.5% of opening active strength transfers to reserve and 2.5% leaves otherwise; disrupted Veylac, Ossavren and Haldrevik use 6% and 4.5%. Separately trained reserve entry is 1.5% of opening reserves. Active intake and reserve eligibility losses reconcile to the adopted closing estimates. These assumptions are not canonical demographic laws. Flows round to 100 people for large forces, 10 for small forces and 5 for Norrakai. Retain the original approximately 15% personnel uncertainty, wider for fragmented returns. Field adjustments reflect transport, fixed duties and training, not a universal manpower multiplier. Preserve this ledger and reconcile a later dated return rather than compounding it or silently relabelling its date.
