The existing description “about sixty-seven years since the Culling” is approximate, not an exact anniversary. The three-year campaign and its established losses remain unchanged. ## Months and clocks

There are twelve numbered months. Months **1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 have 31 days**; all others have **30 days**. Total: **365 local solar days**. No leap day is used in the established civil calendar. Do not substitute Terran month names or assume every month has thirty days.

One mean solar day lasts **24 Terran standard hours and 8 minutes**. One local year is approximately **367.03 Terran standard days**. Local clocks divide a solar day into **24 local hours**, each of 60 local minutes, each of 60 local seconds. One local hour therefore equals 60 Terran standard minutes and 20 seconds. Daylight varies with latitude and season.

Northern winter falls around the beginning of the year; northern summer around midyear. Southern seasons are reversed. Departure on 22/10/0068 R is northern autumn, approximately four months after northern summer solstice. This is a local calendar, not an Imperial galactic date or a universal clock for the Chronostrife.
