# Malaspina — local calendar

One mean solar day (one day/night cycle) lasts **24 hours 8 minutes**, measured in Earth-duration hours. One local year lasts **365 local solar days**, approximately **367.03 Earth days**. These local astronomical periods are close to Earth's. Daylight hours vary with latitude and season; a solar day does not mean 24 hours of daylight.

Local civil clocks divide each solar day into 24 local clock hours, each into 60 local minutes and 60 local seconds. Thus one local clock hour corresponds to 60 minutes 20 seconds in Earth-duration units. Local appointments, transport timetables and elapsed story days use local clocks. Real-world comparisons use Earth-duration units when explicitly labelled. No existing journey, age or scene is rescaled by this clarification.

The calendar has twelve pay months per local year. Their individual lengths, names, the numbered current date and the year number remain unspecified; the 365-day year does not establish a birthday or retroactive payday.
