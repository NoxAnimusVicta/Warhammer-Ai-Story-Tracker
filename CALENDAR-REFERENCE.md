# Malaspina — culling calendar

**Current date: 11/10/0068 AC43, evening.** In speech: Day 11 of Month 10, **68 AC43**. Dates are **day / numbered month / year after the numbered major culling**.

**AC43 means After Culling 43.** The current year is the sixty-eighth year of the era following the last major culling. “About 67 years since its end” is the rounded historical description, not an exact anniversary. The count follows the surviving common reckoning; it does not prove that only 43 cullings have ever occurred.

A recognised major culling starts a new era after its end. The next regular civil new year begins **Year 1 AC44**, preserving the seasonal month cycle. Ordinary hunts, local suppression raids and human wars do not reset the year. During an unfinished major culling, dates retain the existing era until the end is established. An eventual change requires an explicit bridge between old and new dates; never silently relabel historical events.

This replaces the Reconstruction-era notation. It neither invents another catastrophe nor erases older history. Industrial societies, languages, oral traditions and written records persist across eras; repeated attacks and warfare gradually destroy particular knowledge and institutions. See [historical traditions and Ambros](HISTORY-REFERENCE.md).

## Months and clocks

Twelve numbered months total **365 local solar days**. Months **1, 3, 5, 7 and 9 have 31 days**; all other months have **30 days**. No leap day is used in the established civil calendar. Do not substitute Terran month names or assume uniform thirty-day months.

One solar day lasts **24 Terran standard hours and 8 minutes**. Local clocks divide it into **24 local hours**, each of 60 local minutes and 60 local seconds. One local hour equals 60 Terran standard minutes and 20 seconds. A local year is approximately **367.03 Terran standard days**. Daylight varies by latitude and season.

The civil new year falls near northern winter solstice; northern summer is around midyear, with southern seasons reversed. Departure remains northern autumn, approximately four months after northern summer solstice. This local reckoning does not establish an absolute Imperial date or a universal Chronostrife conversion.
